Our Launch Offer

How your 3-hour Launch Offer works.

We're just starting out, so our first 10 households get a special Launch Offer price of $200. In return, we ask for one hour of honest feedback. We want to learn how this works in real kitchens, so we can get it right for every family after you. Three hours is unhurried: time to cook properly, and time to talk while it's on the stove.

In all, about 5 hours with your cook: a 1-hour complimentary meet and greet, a 3-hour cooking day and a 1-hour feedback chat, plus a little time for the call and menu planning.

  1. We phone you

    Book the Launch Offer or ask for a call back. We'll phone you to talk it through.

  2. Complimentary meet and greet at home

    A separate visit before cooking day, with no extra charge. We meet you and your parent face to face, talk through their favourite dishes and how they like to eat, and have a gentle look at the kitchen where we'll be cooking. It's part of the Launch Offer now, and we'd like to make it part of every package, so we're keen to see how it goes.

  3. Choose the menu

    After the meet and greet, we send a few menus the way you prefer to be contacted. You and your parent choose the one you like best.

  4. Sort the groceries

    You choose and buy the groceries, so the $200 is all you pay us for the session. We send a shopping list if you'd like to shop yourself. If you'd rather we shop, that's an optional extra at $60 per hour during the Launch Offer.

  5. Cooking day

    Your cook comes to the home for 3 hours, cooks the way you like it, and tidies up before they go.

  6. Choice of menu and number of meals

    You choose the menu, and the number of meals depends on what you choose. Meals can be stored or shared, with a note on what to eat first and what to freeze.

  7. Feedback chat

    In exchange for the Launch Offer price, you give us an hour of honest feedback, at your home or by video: what worked, what didn't, and what you'd change. We're learning from every kitchen, and it helps us get this right for every family after you.

Before you book

What we do, and what we don't.

We cook

Homestead is a home-cooking service. We cook meals in your home using groceries you choose and buy.

We are not a personal care or nursing service: we don't help with showering, dressing, toileting, moving around, or medications, and we don't give medical or nutrition advice. We are not a registered aged care provider or a dietitian.

If your parent needs that kind of support, their aged care provider or GP is the right place to start. If they have a medical diet or swallowing difficulty, please follow their health professional's instructions and tell us before the first booking.

Allergies and dietary needs

Cooking safely around an allergy takes the two of us. Here is how we share it.

What you do: Tell us about every allergy, intolerance and texture need before the first booking, and tell us if anything changes. You choose and buy the groceries, so please check labels against your parent's list.

What we do: We ask again at every visit. We read labels on what's in the kitchen, and we'll stop and tell you if something doesn't look right.

What we can't promise: A home kitchen can't be made allergen-free, and we can't guarantee it. If your parent has a severe allergy (one that has caused anaphylaxis), please talk to us before you book. Inyoung, at our team.

FAQs

Questions families ask us.

About Homestead

What is Homestead Support?

We're a small Melbourne business. We send a skilled cook into your home, or your parent's, to cook the food you know and love, in your own kitchen, with your own pots, the way you like it. We start with a short list of cuisines and, as we grow, we'll match more cooks and languages.

Is this a meal delivery service?

No. We don't deliver pre-made meals. Your cook comes to the home and cooks fresh there, stores the extra meals, and cleans up afterwards. It sits alongside meal delivery services rather than replacing them.

Who is Homestead for?

Older Australians who want home-cooked food from their own culture, in their own kitchen, and the daughters, sons and families who arrange it for Mum or Dad and worry about whether they're eating well.

Where do you work?

We're starting in Melbourne's north-east, in Banyule and Nillumbik, including Greensborough. If you're somewhere else, contact us anyway and tell us your suburb. We can't promise, but we'd like to know where families need us.

Do you have a cook for our language and cuisine?

Right now Homestead has one cook, and we're growing. Tell us the food your parent loves and the language they speak, and we'll say honestly whether we can match both before you book. If we can't yet, leave your details and we'll let you know when we find the right cook.

The Launch Offer

What's in the Launch Offer?

A phone call, then a complimentary meet and greet at home: we talk through your parent's food and take a gentle look at their kitchen. Afterwards we send you a few menus the way you prefer to be contacted, and you choose the one you like best. You choose and buy the groceries. Then comes a 3-hour cooking session where the cook prepares the menu you chose and tidies up before they go. Finally there's a 1-hour feedback chat with us.

Why is the Launch Offer $200, and why do you ask for feedback?

We're new, and we want to build this with the families who use it. Our first 10 households get the Launch Offer for $200. In return, we ask for one hour of honest feedback about what worked, what didn't, and what you'd change. We're learning from every kitchen we cook in, and your answers shape the service. We'll come to your home for the chat, or we can do it by video if you prefer.

Does my parent need to be home?

Usually, yes. Your parent will see the cook up to three times: the meet and greet, the cooking day and the feedback chat. There's time to talk while the cooking happens, but it's up to your parent how involved they'd like to be. If someone else will let the cook in, just tell us when you book.

Can I arrange this for my mum or dad?

Yes. You can ask for a call back or book on their behalf, and we'll include your parent in the meet and greet.

Food, shopping and meals

Which cuisines do you cook?

For the Launch Offer we cook Modern Australian, Italian, French, Greek and Korean, and we're adding more over time. If your family's food isn't on the list, tell us what they love. Our cook can often make it with your guidance or from recipes you share, or we'll let you know when we find the right cook.

Can the cook make my parent's own recipes?

Yes. That's the point. Your parent can teach the cook a family recipe, choose the dishes, or simply tell us the taste they remember. We'd love them to stay involved in the kitchen if they want to.

Can you cook for special diets?

We cook with nutrition in mind, and we can cook vegetarian, salt-reduced, gluten-free and soft-texture meals. We're not set up to cook for serious or life-threatening allergies, for coeliac-level gluten-free diets, or to prepare pureed food for swallowing difficulties. Tell us about your parent's dietary needs when you book, and we'll talk them through at the consultation. If a dietitian has approved recipes or a meal plan, please share it and we'll cook it as written, provided it stays within the limits above. Those limits apply even if a plan asks for more.

Who buys the groceries?

You choose and buy the groceries, so the $200 is all you pay us for the session. We send a shopping list and you shop, or, if you'd rather, our cook shops for you as an optional extra at $60 per hour during the Launch Offer.

What about the meals in the fridge?

Meals kept in the fridge are best eaten within about 3 days. Anything you won't eat in that time can go in the freezer. Your cook will show your parent what to eat first and what to freeze. We'll talk more about this when we plan the menu.

Can you help organise the fridge, freezer and pantry?

Not as part of the Launch Offer, but you can ask for it in the future. Organising and cleaning a fridge, freezer and pantry takes a good understanding of your household, so it needs to be arranged in advance. Tell us you're interested when you request a call back or book, and we'll talk it through.

Our cooks, cost and contracts

How are your cooks checked?

Our cook is a qualified chef with food safety training and a current NDIS Worker Screening Check. Every cook who joins us will be checked the same way. Qualifications and checks are available on request. Insurance: add the cover and insurer name only once confirmed.

What happens after the Launch Offer, and what does it cost?

If you'd like to continue after the Launch Offer, just tell us. We'll talk through what suits your parent, and the price is adjusted accordingly.

Is there a contract? Do I need to cancel?

No. There's no lock-in contract, so there's nothing to cancel. If you'd like to continue the service after the Launch Offer, just tell us.

Do we need an aged care package to use Homestead?

No. For now, Homestead is a private service, so anyone can book without an assessment. If you'd like to explore government support for your parent, start with My Aged Care on 1800 200 422 or myagedcare.gov.au.
